Before every gym session

Mobility warm-up

8 to 10 minutes · dynamic preparation

0 / 7
Prepare, do not fatigue.

Use dynamic mobility before lifting. Save long static holds for after training. Then perform ramp-up sets for the first 1 to 2 compound lifts.

Easy bike, incline walk, or rower

3 min

Raise body temperature at an easy pace.

Ankle rocks

8 / side

Move the knee forward over the toes while keeping the heel down.

90/90 hip transitions

6 / side

Rotate slowly through the hips. Keep the movement controlled.

World's greatest stretch

4 / side

Use it dynamically. Do not turn it into a long passive hold.

Band pull-aparts

15 reps

Control the shoulder blades and avoid shrugging.

Scapular push-ups

10 reps

Protract and retract the shoulder blades without bending the elbows.

Ramp-up sets

2 to 4 sets

For the first compound lift, progressively increase load before the working sets.

After lifting

Stretching reset

6 to 8 minutes · choose 4 to 6 positions

0 / 6
30 to 45 seconds per position.

Use calm breathing and controlled tension, not pain. One round is enough on busy days. Use two rounds when flexibility is a priority.

Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch

30 to 45 s / side

Tuck the pelvis gently and keep the ribs stacked.

Glute stretch

30 to 45 s / side

Use controlled tension without forcing the knee or hip.

Relaxed hamstring stretch

30 to 45 s / side

Keep it gentle. Do not chase maximum range after hard RDLs.

Calf wall stretch

30 to 45 s / side

Keep the heel down and foot pointing mostly forward.

Doorway pec stretch

30 to 45 s / side

Keep the shoulder relaxed and avoid sharp anterior shoulder pain.

Overhead lat stretch

30 to 45 s / side

Breathe into the side of the ribcage and avoid excessive low-back arch.
